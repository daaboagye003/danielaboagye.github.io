Selected work

Research projects

Long-term no-tillage & residue-derived carbon

My PhD research examined how long-term no-tillage and residue application methods regulate the distribution and accumulation of residue-derived carbon in soil organic matter fractions in Japanese Andosols.

Residue-derived carbon & soil organic matter

Experimental work using isotopically labelled plant residues to trace carbon through particulate and mineral-associated soil organic matter pools over time and depth.

Sustainable agricultural inputs

Research on organic amendments and nitrogen management, including biochar–compost strategies and their implications for crop production and soil fertility.

Biodegradable organic mulch materials

Field research evaluating an organic mulch-board material for lettuce production, including interactions with algal biomass and plant growth and physiological traits.

Methods

Research methods & tools
